A Longmont Saturday Tradition

Longmont Farmers Market

The Longmont Farmers Market brings together more than 100 Colorado farmers, ranchers, food producers and local vendors at the Boulder County Fairgrounds.

It is a growers-only market, meaning the food is grown, raised or produced locally and sold by the people who make it. Live music, children's activities and community gathering are also part of the market experience.

📍 Location:
Boulder County Fairgrounds
9595 Nelson Road, Longmont
📅 2026 Season:
Saturdays, April 4 – November 21
🕐 Hours: 8 a.m.–1 p.m.
🎨 Artisan Market: Fourth Saturday of the month, April through October.
🥬 Food Access: SNAP/EBT and eligible food-access programs are supported.

Longmont Food Rescue

More Ways to Belong

Community Doesn't Have to Be Complicated

Sometimes connection starts with something simple: buying vegetables from a farmer, volunteering for an afternoon, joining a class or introducing yourself to someone new.

Buy Local Food

Shopping from local farms and growers keeps more money in the local food system and creates connections between residents and the people who grow their food.

Volunteer

Food pantries, senior programs, libraries, parks and local nonprofits all depend on people willing to give a little time.

Show Up

Markets, classes, community events, faith communities and local groups are easier places to meet people than waiting for community to find you.
