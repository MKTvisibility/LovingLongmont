Send Us a Message

Fill this out and your email program will open with the information ready to send to Loving Longmont.

This version does not store your information on the Loving Longmont website. Clicking the button opens your email program so you can send the message directly.

Helpful Details

What Should I Include?

The more information you send, the easier it will be for us to review and share it accurately.

📅

Event Submissions

Include the event name, date, time, location, description, cost if applicable, website or registration link, and an image if available.

🏪

Business Submissions

Include the business name, location, website, social media links, contact information and a brief description of what the business offers.

💼

Job Submissions

Include the employer, job title, location, pay range if available, employment type, short description and official application link.

📷

Photo Submissions

Include where the photo was taken, your name exactly as you want the photo credit displayed, and any information you'd like us to know about it.

📷
