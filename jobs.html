Workforce Boulder County

Workforce Boulder County is one of the most useful employment resources available to Longmont residents. Their Longmont office welcomes walk-ins and can help with much more than simply finding job postings.

  • Career coaching and job-search assistance
  • Résumé and interview guidance
  • Career workshops
  • Computers and resource-room access for job searches
  • Self-guided computer-skills training
  • Education and training resources
  • Veterans services
  • Young adult employment programs
  • Connecting Colorado assistance

Job Search Safety

Unfortunately, job seekers are common targets for scams. A few precautions can help protect you.

  • Be cautious if a company offers you a job without a real interview or meaningful conversation.
  • Never pay an employer for the privilege of getting a job.
  • Do not purchase gift cards, cryptocurrency or equipment for a supposed employer and expect reimbursement later.
  • Verify that the employer's email address matches its official company website whenever possible.
  • Be suspicious of unusually high pay for very simple work, especially unsolicited remote-job offers.
  • Never give banking, Social Security or identity information until you have verified the employer and reached the legitimate hiring/onboarding stage.
  • When in doubt, find the company's official website yourself instead of clicking the link in a suspicious message.
