The Loving Longmont Guide

Explore Longmont

From free outdoor adventures to memorable experiences, there is a lot more to do around Longmont than you may realize.

Golden Ponds Nature Area

Four ponds, mountain views, wildlife and 2.6 miles of trails make Golden Ponds one of Longmont's easiest places to spend a peaceful Colorado afternoon.

View Business Page →

McIntosh Lake Nature Area

Enjoy Longs Peak views while walking around the lake, fishing, kayaking, canoeing or paddleboarding. It's one of Longmont's favorite sunset spots.

View Business Page →

Dickens Farm Nature Area

St. Vrain Creek runs directly through this 52-acre nature area. Explore the nature playground, beginner bike-skills trail, slower-moving water areas and St. Vrain Greenway.

View Business Page →

Union Reservoir Nature Area

This 736-acre body of water offers boating, fishing, kayaking, paddleboarding, picnicking and a dedicated dog beach.

View Business Page →

Agricultural Heritage Center

Discover Boulder County's agricultural history while exploring the historic farm, red barn, equipment, rural landscape and seasonal farm animals.

View Business Page →

Sandstone Ranch Visitors & Learning Center

Explore a historic homestead first settled in 1860. Inside you'll find hands-on history and environmental exhibits, while outside trails pass historic buildings, sandstone bluffs and wildlife habitat.

View Business Page →

Longmont Museum

Explore exhibitions about Longmont's history, art and culture and enjoy performances, films, talks, classes and special community events.

View Business Page →

Sunflower Farm

FARMFEST public hours are open each weekend, where you will be able to have direct contact with all of your favorite farm animals and can visit our pumpkin patch, pick your own sunflower fields and visit our Halloween Forest in October. During Farmfest you will discover an old airplane, sandboxes, bicycles, tree houses, playhouses, dueling zip-lines, swings and John Deere Tractors.

View Business Page →

The Bee Hugger Farm

A quirky six-acre family farm with bees, horses, goats, pigs, chickens, peacocks, U-Pick sunflowers, pumpkins, local honey and more. Visitors can feed animals, climb tractors and explore the property. Check the website and social media for pony rides, storytimes and other special events.

View Business Page →

Longmont Climbing Collective

Try indoor climbing, bouldering, fitness or yoga. Programs are available for adults and children, making it a great year-round activity.

View Business Page →

Longmont Recreation Center

Swim, climb, play basketball or work out under one roof. The center includes lap and leisure pools, waterslides, a lazy river, climbing wall, gymnasium and fitness areas.

View Business Page →

Mile-Hi Skydiving Center

For a completely different view of Longmont, take to the sky. Mile-Hi offers tandem skydives for first-timers as well as training for experienced jumpers.

View Business Page →

Longmont Ice Pavilion

The open-air covered rink at Roosevelt Park offers public skating, hockey and lessons during the winter season.

View Business Page →

Wells Athletics Batting Cages

A locally owned, year-round indoor baseball facility with batting cages, pitching machines and space for individual or team practice.

View Business Page →

Longmont Baseball League Cages

The nonprofit Longmont Baseball League rents two indoor cages with an Iron Mike pitching machine, hitting nets, tees and practice balls.

View Business Page →

Garden Acres Community Park

A neighborhood sports hub with a batting cage, lighted softball fields, soccer space, cricket pitch, playgrounds, paths and public art.

View Business Page →

Crackpots

A downtown paint-your-own-pottery studio offering glass fusing, mosaics, classes, parties and walk-in creative time for all ages.

View Business Page →

Atomic Goblin Games

An independent game shop and gathering place for board games, trading cards, tabletop miniatures and scheduled community play.

View Business Page →

TinkerMill

A volunteer-powered nonprofit makerspace with woodworking, welding, pottery, textiles, robotics, laser cutting, blacksmithing and more.

View Business Page →

YA YA Farm & Orchard

A historic family orchard with fruit trees, farm animals, a seasonal farm stand and reservation-based U-pick experiences.

View Business Page →

Firehouse Art Center

Longmont’s nonprofit contemporary art center presents rotating exhibitions, education programs and community events in the Creative District.

View Business Page →
Choose Your Adventure

What Kind of Day Are You Looking For?

A Completely Free Day

Walk around McIntosh Lake, explore Golden Ponds, visit the Agricultural Heritage Center or discover Longmont history at Sandstone Ranch.

A Family Day

Explore Dickens Farm, meet the animals at Sunflower Farm or spend the afternoon swimming at the Longmont Recreation Center.

A Date Day

Take a sunset walk at Golden Ponds or McIntosh Lake, try climbing together or ice skate during the winter.

A Water Day

Kayak or paddleboard at McIntosh Lake or spend a full summer day at Union Reservoir.

A Rainy Day

Try the Longmont Climbing Collective, Recreation Center or check the Longmont Museum's program schedule.

A Bucket-List Day

Go skydiving over the Front Range or try something new like climbing, kayaking or paddleboarding.

Before You Go

  • Hours, prices, programs and seasonal availability can change. Use the official website button on each listing before making a special trip.
  • Most Longmont nature areas are open approximately one hour before sunrise until one hour after sunset.
  • A Colorado Parks & Wildlife fishing license is required at Longmont fishing locations when applicable.
  • Water levels and creek conditions vary throughout the year. Always follow posted safety information.
  • Many outdoor Longmont attractions are completely free, making it possible to explore the community without spending anything.
