Our Story

Why Loving Longmont

Loving Longmont began in 2021, not long after I moved to Longmont.

I was new to the community, and I created Loving Longmont because I wanted a place to discover and share the things that make Longmont such a great place to live.

In September 2026, an unexpected corporate layoff changed my direction. It gave me the desire to take what I had learned throughout my working years and use that experience to help small businesses. Owning a business and working for myself had also been a dream of mine for a long time.

Instead of simply moving on to another corporate position, I partnered with my son Tim Skolyak to start MKT Visibility, a family-owned marketing business focused on helping small businesses get noticed, connect with customers and grow.

Community + Local Business

Why These Two Projects Belong Together

The world can feel pretty complicated sometimes, but one thing we can influence is what happens right here in our own community.

I believe communities are stronger when local businesses thrive, neighbors support one another, and residents get out and experience the place they call home.

That's where Loving Longmont and MKT Visibility work together.

Loving Longmont

Loving Longmont is for the community. It's a place to discover local businesses, restaurants, events, activities, jobs, nonprofits, animals, history, outdoor spaces, and the people and stories that make Longmont unique.

MKT Visibility

MKT Visibility is for the businesses behind the community. We help small businesses become easier to find and give owners support with marketing, websites, social media, content, photography, and their online presence.

For residents, we hope you'll use this site to discover more of Longmont and support the people and businesses around you.

For business owners, we hope Loving Longmont gives you another place to be discovered—and if you want to see how your business appears to potential customers online, MKT Visibility offers a free Business Visibility Check.
