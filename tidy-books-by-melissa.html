Tidy Books by Melissa

Tidy Books by Melissa provides monthly bookkeeping, financial reporting, catch-up and cleanup services, and QuickBooks Online and Desktop support for small businesses and individuals. Melissa works with accountants and tax preparers to help clients keep accurate, organized financial records.

Services

  • Monthly bookkeeping and account reconciliation
  • Financial reporting
  • Catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping
  • QuickBooks setup, training and ongoing support

Meet Melissa

Melissa founded Tidy Books by Melissa to help small-business owners maintain clear, organized books without unnecessary stress. She offers a free 20-minute bookkeeping checkup.
