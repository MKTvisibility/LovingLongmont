How do I get started?

Choose your option above and send us your business name and contact details. We will match your request to your existing listing and coordinate payment through Square. The request buttons do not charge you or start a subscription.

What does a one-time update include?

The $45 option covers one consolidated update to your existing business profile. Send your requested changes together. It does not include ongoing featured placement or recurring event submissions.

How many updates and events are included in membership?

Both quarterly and annual memberships include up to two business profile update requests and up to two event submissions per month. Send the changes for each profile update together in one request. Annual payment provides the same monthly service limits as quarterly payment.

How do I send updates?

Send your changes and event details through our contact page. Submissions are reviewed before publication. Featured placement is clearly labeled and may be shared with other members.

What if my business moves or needs an urgent change?

Urgent business updates, such as a move or another essential change customers need to know about, are included with membership and do not count toward your two monthly profile updates.

Can you post a temporary closure notice?

Yes. If your business will be closed for a week or longer, including for a vacation or an unexpected extended closure, membership includes a notice for that time period. Send us the closure dates and expected reopening date so the notice covers the correct period. These notices are included in addition to your monthly profile updates. Closure notices for only one or a few days are not included.

How does billing work?

The one-time update is $45 with no recurring charge. Membership is $125 billed every three months or $450 billed annually. Monthly equivalents are shown for comparison; membership is not billed monthly. Recurring subscriptions will be handled through Square, with renewal and cancellation terms shown before payment.

Where are my payment details stored?

Payment details are handled by Square. We do not collect card details on this page or store payment information in Command Center.

What results can I expect?

Membership provides the visibility and support listed above. Sales, inquiries and search rankings are not guaranteed.
