Your support keeps Longmont visible.

Your support helps us discover businesses, maintain profiles, tell local stories, create community content, and promote Loving Longmont both online and offline.

Already listed? You're part of our community.

If you received a welcome email from Loving Longmont, save it and add us to your contacts. It means we thought your business or professional story belonged here. Your free profile includes one requested routine update each year, plus urgent factual corrections or announcements as needed, subject to review.

Business pages and professional pages are both welcome. Locally owned businesses can share their business stories. Longmont residents who work for larger companies—including real estate agents, tax advisors, sales representatives and other specialists—can have free individual professional pages featuring their experience, services, what they love about their work and how they help others. You don't need a storefront or a large business to deserve recognition. We especially love discovering entrepreneurs, crafters, makers, home-based and cottage-food businesses, porch bakers selling treats like cake pops, and people building a small business or side hustle to support their families and contribute to the community. We also welcome personal professional pages for Longmont musicians, artists, writers and self-published authors, including those using Amazon KDP. Whether you have a storefront, sell at local markets, work from home or are just getting started, we want to help neighbors discover the unique people and small ventures around them. Employers and affiliations can be mentioned, while the spotlight stays on the local person and their story.

Sponsors support the broader community. Profile update limits refer to consolidated requests; major redesigns and separate marketing services are not included. Contributions are not purchases of favorable editorial coverage.

Your Sponsorship Makes a Real Difference ❤️

Behind every business profile, community story, event listing, and local discovery are hours of research, writing, photography, website maintenance, and promotion. That's why your sponsorship matters so much.

Your support gives us more time to discover and showcase the people, businesses, professionals, artists, and organizations that make Longmont unique.

As a community sponsor, you also receive opportunities to help your own business or professional presence grow:

  • Greater visibility: Sponsor recognition helps introduce your business or professional services to our community.
  • A stronger online presence: Keep your profile updated with your services, photos, contact information, and important announcements, within your sponsorship's update allowance.
  • Opportunities to share your story: Help people understand who you are, what you do, and what makes you special.
  • Community connections: Join a growing network encouraging residents and visitors to discover and support local people.
  • Ongoing community promotion: Your sponsorship supports Loving Longmont's website, social media, community outreach, and future projects.

When you sponsor Loving Longmont, you're not just supporting a website. You're helping build a stronger community while giving your own business or professional story another opportunity to be discovered.

Together, we keep Longmont visible.

Recognition and editorial opportunities do not guarantee specific posts, leads, customers, or sales.
