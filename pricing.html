Simple plans. Clear expectations.

How do I get started?

Choose a plan and send your business name and contact details using the request button. We will confirm your listing, collect your content and arrange payment before publishing paid features. Your request does not charge your card or enroll you automatically.

What if I do not have a website?

Your free listing can show your address or service area and phone. An Enhanced or Featured Listing gives you a dedicated Loving Longmont page to share your services, hours, photos and contact information.

Is claiming my business free?

Yes. Confirming ownership and correcting basic business information are free. Paid plans add the features listed above. Please use a business email or provide information that helps us verify your connection to the business.

How do annual plans work?

Paid features run for 12 months from activation. There is no monthly charge and no automatic renewal at this stage. We will contact you before renewal; if you do not renew, your listing returns to the free format.

What counts as a content update?

One consolidated request to change your description, services, hours, links or photos counts as one update. Basic factual corrections remain free. You supply photos you have permission to use. Promotional post timing and featured placement are arranged with our team; featured placement may be shared with other featured businesses.

What results can I expect?

Your plan purchases the presentation and promotion described here. Customer inquiries, sales and search rankings are not guaranteed. Paid features are labeled clearly.
