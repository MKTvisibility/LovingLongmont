Free Food • Food Rescue

Longmont Food Rescue

A local nonprofit reducing food waste while getting fresh food directly to people who can use it. Programs include no-cost groceries, free produce distributions, community fridges and Fresh Food Connect.

Produce Distributions: Free food distributions are held regularly with no questions asked.
Community Fridges: Free community refrigerators allow neighbors to give or take food.
