Four Historic Places to Know

These properties are historic resources rather than regular drop-in attractions. Open hours, tours and interior access vary, so use the official links or call the Historical Society before making a special trip.

Historic Home • National Register District

Historic Hoverhome

The 1913–14 home of Charles Lewis Hover and Katherine Baird Hover anchors a preserved historic district connected to one of Longmont’s influential early families.

📍 Address: 1309 Hover Street, Longmont, CO 80501
☎ Property: 303-774-7810
☎ Historical Society: 303-776-1870
🎟 Access: Public tours are scheduled on select dates; advance tickets or arrangements may be required.
Official Website
Farm History • Agricultural Heritage

Hover Farmstead

The remaining farm buildings preserve the working side of the Hover family property and help tell Longmont’s agricultural story beyond the walls of Hoverhome.

📍 Address: 1303 Hover Road, Longmont, CO 80501
☎ Historical Society: 303-776-1870
🎟 Access: Tours and property access are arranged through the St. Vrain Historical Society.
Official Website
Historic Park • Pioneer Buildings

Old Mill Park

A small downtown historic park where relocated pioneer-era buildings, gardens and artifacts create an approachable window into early life in the St. Vrain Valley.

📍 Address: 237 Pratt Street, Longmont, CO 80501
☎ Historical Society: 303-776-1870
🕐 Access: Seasonal hours and private closures can change; docent tours generally require reservations.
Official Website
1881 Church • Local Landmark

Old St. Stephen’s Church

Built in 1881 and long known as the “Little White Church,” this downtown landmark is among Longmont’s oldest surviving church buildings and now serves community and preservation uses.

📍 Address: 470 Main Street, Longmont, CO 80501
☎ Historical Society: 303-776-1870
🎟 Access: Interior visits are by appointment; use the official property page to request access.
Official Website

Confirm Before You Visit

The St. Vrain Historical Society manages all four properties. For tour availability, accessibility questions, event closures or appointments, call 303-776-1870 or visit the official property page.
